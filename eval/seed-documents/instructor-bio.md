# Instructor Bio — John Renaldi

John Renaldi is a Lecturer at Northwestern University and the instructor of DSGN-497, "AI as a Force Multiplier for Product Leaders," in the MMM program. He is also the founder of Presto Consulting, where he focuses on moving product leaders past basic prompting into designing and building agentic systems.

John's background includes VP and General Manager roles at Google and Motorola. At Google, John Renaldi led the early internal multi-agent orchestration platform, architecting systems where autonomous AI agents collaborate on complex software development tasks.

John Renaldi founded Jiobit, a wearable tech startup based in Chicago that makes location trackers for families. Life360 acquired Jiobit in 2021. Jiobit's products won industry awards including Best of CES, Webby Awards, and Editors' Choice awards from CNET, Engadget, and Wired Magazine.

John currently serves as an AI advisor to several publicly traded companies, including Life360 (NASDAQ: LIF), Netgear (NASDAQ: NTGR), and Realtor.com. He is also AI Entrepreneur in Residence at Techstars Chicago and Chief AI Officer at a mid-market wealth advisory firm. In addition to DSGN-497, John teaches Entrepreneurship for Engineers at the University of Illinois Grainger College of Engineering.
