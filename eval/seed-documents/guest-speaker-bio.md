# Guest Speaker — Elie-Joe Haikal

Elie-Joe Haikal is a Senior AI Software Engineer at Google Cloud. He is the confirmed guest speaker for Module 3 of DSGN-497 on May 21, 2026, where he delivers the opening lecture on eval-driven development.

Elie-Joe Haikal brings a hands-on engineering perspective on how production teams build, ship, and maintain AI systems where reliability is non-negotiable. He is a McGill University computer software engineering graduate. Before joining Google, Elie-Joe Haikal held senior engineering roles at Morgan Stanley, Amazon Web Services, and PariPassu.
