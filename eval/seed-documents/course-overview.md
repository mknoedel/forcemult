# Course Overview — DSGN-497: AI as a Force Multiplier for Product Leaders

DSGN-497 is taught by John Renaldi at Northwestern University in Spring 2026 (May 7 to June 4). The class meets in the Hive classroom at the Ford Design Center. Office hours are Mondays from 8:00 PM to 9:00 PM on Zoom.

Class schedule: Module 1 kicks off Thursday, May 7, 2026, 6:30 PM to 9:30 PM. Module 2 meets Thursday, May 14, 2026. Module 3 meets Thursday, May 21, 2026 and features a guest lecture by Elie-Joe Haikal. Module 4 meets Wednesday, May 27, 2026 (an off-cycle Wednesday meeting). Module 5, the capstone session, meets Thursday, June 4, 2026, 6:30 PM to 9:30 PM.

Grading: the Final Project (a working agent or AI-powered product, recorded video demo plus architecture packet) is worth 50% of the grade. Weekly labs and homework are worth 40% (four labs at 10% each). Participation is worth 10%. The final project deadline is June 12, 2026 at 12pm noon.

The final project has two paths. Path A is a simple chat app built with Claude Code and n8n. Path B is an agentic software build using Claude Code, n8n, and a knowledge graph powered by Zep, with a data ingestion pipeline and two channels: an app and email.

The course uses a flipped classroom format. Required textbook: Co-Intelligence by Ethan Mollick. Suggested reference: AI Engineering by Chip Huyen. The course technology stack includes n8n (the Northwestern workspace), Claude Code, Supabase, OpenRouter, Llama Cloud, Composio, and Zep for knowledge graphs.
